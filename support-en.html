Support

If something goes wrong, email Duo Browser support. Please include your device model, OS and app versions, the website address, and where the problem occurred. Hide sign-in details and personal information before sharing a screenshot.

Bring back the address bar

Scroll up or tap the compact address bar. To move it, choose Top or Bottom under Address Bar Position in the page menu.

Pick up where you left off

In a regular tab, choose Save Reading Spot in the page menu. Use Saved Reading Spots to reopen the page. If the page layout changes, it may reopen near the spot you saved.
