Duo BrowserSupport

Privacy Policy

The developer does not collect or send your browsing history, searches, tabs, bookmarks, or downloads to a developer server. The app has no sign-up, advertising SDK, or usage analytics SDK.

Information stored on your device

Regular tab addresses, bookmarks, your reading list, browsing history, saved reading spots, your search engine, and display settings stay in the app's local storage. A saved reading spot includes the page address, title, and position. WebKit manages cookies and website storage. Files you choose to download are saved to the app's downloads list.

Websites and searches

When you request a page, the website may process your IP address, browser details, and activity on that site. When you run a search from the address bar, your query goes to the search provider you chose. Each website and search provider handles that information under its own privacy policy.

Private tabs

Private tabs use temporary WebKit storage. Visits in those tabs are kept out of regular browsing history, and private tabs are not restored when you relaunch the app. Websites can still see connection information. Files you download, links you share, and items you add to the library may remain until you remove them.

Keeping and deleting information

You can clear visits from History in the Library. Delete bookmarks, reading list entries, reading spots, and downloads from their respective lists. Deleting the app removes its local data. Delete copies saved or shared in other apps from those locations.
