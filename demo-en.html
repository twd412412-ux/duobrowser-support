A Seoul walking notebook

A Slow Afternoon in Seoul

Take the long way along the Han River, then follow the quiet streets uphill as the light goes soft.

Start by the river

01 · Riverside

When the river catches the light

At first the Han is the color of a spoon. Then the sun slips below the clouds and the water turns green and gold. Pick the path closest to the bank. The breeze is cooler there, and you can hear the water between passing bikes.

I stop at the next bench and watch a boat leave a wake so small it disappears before it reaches the shore.

Read about the side streets · See the garden path

02 · Side streets

Turn uphill at the stone steps

Leave the river path at the stairs and take the lane that bends left. Behind the low walls are pots of rosemary, a stack of old tiles, and a bicycle whose basket has filled with leaves. The streets get narrower, and the city sounds farther away.

Where the pavement levels off, look back. You can still see a thin strip of river between the roofs.

03 · The garden

One last loop through the garden

In the garden, seed heads lean over the path and the stone seats are still warm. I take the outer loop once more, then wait for the lamps to come on. There is just enough light left to walk back without hurrying.

You’ve reached the end of the walk.
